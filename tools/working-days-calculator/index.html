How working days are counted

The calculator steps through every date from start to end inclusive, classifies each by its day of the week, and subtracts the days that fall on your chosen weekend — then subtracts any public holidays you enter.

Why a configurable weekend matters

Most online business-day calculators hard-code Saturday and Sunday, which quietly produces wrong answers for a large part of the world. Weekend patterns actually in use include:

PatternWhere
Saturday & SundayEurope, the Americas, most of Asia, and the UAE public sector since 2022 (which moved to a half-day Friday)
Friday & SaturdayEgypt, Jordan, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, Saudi Arabia, and many UAE private-sector employers
Friday onlySome single-day-weekend employers across South Asia and the Middle East
Thursday & FridayThe historic Gulf pattern, still found in some legacy contracts

Pick the pattern in your own contract. If your employer runs a half-day Friday, count it as a working day and adjust the hours separately.

Inclusive counting

Both endpoints are included, so 1 March to 31 March returns 31 calendar days. This matches how notice periods, probation and leave are normally counted in employment contracts. If you need the gap between two dates rather than the span, subtract one.

Public holidays

Holiday dates differ by country and several — including Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha — move each year with the lunar calendar, so they are entered as a number rather than guessed. Count only the holidays that fall on days you would otherwise have worked; a holiday landing on your weekend does not add a day off.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator include both the start and end date?

Yes. A range from 1 March to 31 March counts as 31 calendar days. This matches standard employment-contract practice for notice and leave periods.

Which weekend should I choose for the UAE?

It depends on the employer. The UAE public sector moved to Saturday–Sunday with a half-day Friday in January 2022, but many private-sector companies kept Friday–Saturday. Check your own contract.

Why enter holidays as a number instead of picking a country?

Because public holidays vary by emirate, state and province, and Islamic holidays shift each year with moon sighting — often confirmed only days in advance. A number you control is more accurate than a stale built-in list.

Can I use this for a notice period?

Yes, but check what your contract counts. Most notice periods run in calendar days, not working days, so the total-calendar-days figure is usually the relevant one.

How many working days are in a year?

About 260 on a five-day week before public holidays — 365 days minus 104 weekend days. Subtract your local holidays, typically 8 to 15, for the real figure.
