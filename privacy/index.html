Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your inputs

All calculators run entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter into a calculator are never sent to our servers and are not stored by us.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (property G-2XXGD6ZV2F) to count visits and see which pages are useful. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address, browser details and the pages you view; it does not receive the numbers you type into a calculator. We do not currently show a cookie consent banner. You can block Google Analytics with your browser settings or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. We do not currently show advertising on Calcnate.

Contact form and email

If you use the contact form while online sending is switched on, the name, email address, optional phone number, subject and message you enter, plus the page you sent it from, are passed through a Google Apps Script and delivered to our inbox by Gmail (Google). If online sending is off or fails, the form opens your own email app and nothing is sent until you send the email yourself. We use your message to reply to you.
